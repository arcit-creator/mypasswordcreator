When to use a passphrase

Use one where you have to type it yourself: your computer login, your password manager's master password, a Wi-Fi network, or an account on a TV. For everything else, a password manager filling in a random password is simpler.

Typing on a TV remote or game controller? Choose Short words. Every word is 3 to 5 letters, so there are fewer letters to hunt for. Use 8 short words to match the strength of 6 standard ones.

The strength comes from the words being picked at random, not from you choosing them. A phrase you make up, like a song lyric, is much weaker because attackers try common phrases first.
