About MyPasswordCreator

A free password generator built on one rule: the passwords it makes are none of our business.

MyPasswordCreator is built and run by an independent IT consultant who sets up accounts, networks, and systems for small businesses.

How it's built:

  • Passwords are created in your browser with its cryptographically secure random number generator, never on a server.
  • There are no accounts, cookies, ads, or tracking scripts.
  • After the first visit, it works with no internet connection at all.
  • The site is plain static files with a strict security policy, so there's nothing on our side that could collect a password.
  • Strength estimates use a pessimistic attacker, not a flattering one.
  • Advice follows current NIST guidance rather than folklore.
