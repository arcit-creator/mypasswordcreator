Privacy and terms

Short version: we never see the passwords you generate or check, and we don't track you.

Last updated {{buildDate}}

Passwords and passphrases

Everything you generate is created by code running in your browser. It isn't sent to us or anyone else, and we have no way to see it. The "Copied this session" list is held in the page's memory and is erased when you close or reload the tab.

Breach check

When you check a password, your browser computes its SHA-1 hash and sends only the first 5 characters of that hash to the Have I Been Pwned Pwned Passwords service, which is run by a third party. Like any website, that service receives your IP address with the request. It can't determine your password from 5 hash characters. Files you audit are read in your browser and never uploaded.

What's stored on your device

We save your generator settings (like length and character types) in your browser's local storage, so they're the same next time. No passwords are stored. You can erase these by clearing this site's data in your browser.

So the site works offline, your browser also keeps a copy of this site's own pages and files. It contains nothing you generated or typed. Clearing this site's data removes it.

We don't use cookies.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like every web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address and basic request information (such as which page was requested and your browser type) to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

We use no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. If that ever changes, this page will be updated first and will say exactly what was added.
