Changing your Wi-Fi password

Sign in to your router's admin page or app, open the wireless or Wi-Fi settings, and paste the new password into the field usually called password, passphrase, or pre-shared key. Every device will need to reconnect with the new one, which is where the card helps.

Which style should I use?

  • Short words for a home or guest network people type in: easy to read out loud and to enter on a TV or game console.
  • Easy characters when a device's keyboard makes switching to words awkward: lowercase letters and numbers only, with no look-alikes.
  • Maximum for a network devices join by QR code or are set up once: 63 random characters, the most Wi-Fi allows.

Wi-Fi passwords can be attacked offline: anyone in range can capture the connection handshake and keep guessing at home. That's why length matters here as much as anywhere. Use a separate guest network for visitors when your router offers one.
