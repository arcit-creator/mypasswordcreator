Try the airplane-mode test

  1. Open the app, or this site, once while you're online.
  2. Turn on airplane mode, or unplug from the internet.
  3. Generate passwords, passphrases, PINs, and Wi-Fi cards. Everything still works.

The one exception is the breach check, which has to ask Have I Been Pwned and needs a connection.

What the app is

  • The same site, saved. Not a separate download from an app store. Your browser keeps a copy of these pages and opens them in their own window.
  • Updates itself. When you're online, it picks up the latest version automatically.
  • Easy to remove. Delete it like any other app. Nothing else is installed.
